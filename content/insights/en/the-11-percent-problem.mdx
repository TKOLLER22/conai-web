Eurostat's numbers show a near four-fold adoption gap between large and small businesses in the EU. The usual explanation is budgets. The real one is bandwidth: nobody in a ten-person company has a spare week to evaluate tooling.

But the gap cuts both ways. When your competitors are this slow, a single well-chosen automation (invoice intake, customer triage, document search) moves you ahead of most of your market.

The trick is choosing well. That's why we start every engagement with an audit instead of a toolbox: where the hours leak decides what gets built.
